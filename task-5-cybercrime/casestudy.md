# Case Study: UPI / Online Payment Fraud

The fraud related to UPI is quite common in India, and most often, it affects those who use apps like Google Pay or PhonePe. It occurs when fraudsters dupe a person into sending money without their knowledge.

For example, a student might receive a call or message from an individual who claims to be a bank employee or an online buyer. The caller or sender might claim to be sending money and ask the student to allow them to do so. The student might think that he or she is receiving money, but in fact, by entering the PIN, he or she is sending money from their account.


At other times, fraudsters might send links or a QR code and ask the person to scan them. This, too, results in money being sent from the account.


This demonstrates that we should never enter our PIN to receive money and should not believe what fraudsters say.
